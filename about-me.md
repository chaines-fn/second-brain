# About Me — Chuck Haines

## Role & Title
**Sr. Principal Software Engineer** at **Field Nation**
Department: Software Engineering

## What Field Nation Does
Field Nation is an on-site labor marketplace connecting field service companies with skilled IT technicians. The platform enables companies to access vetted technical talent for networking, cabling, POS installations, and more — serving 7,000+ companies and completing over 1 million work orders annually across the U.S. and Canada.

## What I Do Day-to-Day
I wear a lot of hats. My primary focus areas:

- **Architecture & technical design** — Leading architecture decisions and driving technical design across teams
- **POCs & proving ideas** — Building proof-of-concepts to validate new approaches and technologies
- **Mentoring** — Helping engineers grow through direct guidance and internal knowledge sharing
- **Code review** — Reviewing pull requests from other engineers
- **Hands-on coding** — Especially for POCs and proving out ideas

I don't lead a team directly — my influence is cross-cutting across multiple teams.
