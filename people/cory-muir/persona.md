# Persona: Cory Muir

- **Role:** Sr. Director, Software Engineering
- **Relationship:** Chuck's direct manager
- **Use case:** Draft Slack messages to him; anticipate his feedback; write in his voice
- **Last updated:** 2026-06-29
- **Built from:** ~60 Slack messages (DMs, group DMs, team channel, public announcements)

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Formality:** Highly context-dependent — raw and fragmented in DMs/group DMs, warm and structured in public announcements
- **Sentence length:** Very short in private; streams thoughts as multiple back-to-back messages rather than one long one
- **Formatting:** No formatting in DMs; bullets in longer informational posts; no headers
- **Hedging:** Asserts directly. Rarely qualifies. States opinions early.
- **Emoji/punctuation:** Light emoji use (reactions, :pikachu-surprised: for humor); exclamation points in public/broadcast messages; "..." and "---" as trailing separators in DMs; occasional profanity in private contexts

---

## Signature Patterns

- **Slack opener (DMs):** Dives straight in — no greeting, no context-setting
- **Slack closer (DMs):** Ends with a question, action item, or just stops — no sign-off
- **Slack opener (public/broadcast):** Energetic opener — "Happy Claude Day!!!", "<!here>" + context
- **Slack closer (public):** Enthusiastic send-off — "Happy Clauding!!"
- **Action assignment:** Names the person directly: "So Chuck, action items for us..."

---

## Point of View & Priorities

- **Anti-over-planning** — Prefers iteration and reforecasting over locking in plans that will be wrong. Consistently pushes back on detailed planning for high-uncertainty work. ("stop planning for shit that is 90% gonna change")
- **Alignment before execution** — Surfaces ambiguity upward to leadership before diving in; won't let teams plow ahead when direction is unclear
- **AI/technology forward** — Active champion of AI tooling adoption internally; personally running rollouts, tracking use cases, and pushing experimentation
- **Practical about resources** — Thinks carefully about budget, hiring slots, vendor pricing; looks for angles to cut costs or maximize value
- **Org structure thinker** — Detailed about team compositions, reporting lines, and role definitions; proactively plans hiring sequencing
- **Disagreement style:** States it plainly and directly. Doesn't go quiet. ("I'm sure this all made sense to someone… somewhere… but who was guiding the ship here??")

---

## Known Stances

- Less planning + constant reforecasting > detailed upfront plans (especially in high-change environments)
- Leadership feedback on progress > tracking to a plan

---

## How to Write *To* Him

- Lead with the point — no preamble
- Short is better; he disengages from long messages
- If you need a decision, make the options explicit ("Lane 1 vs Lane 2") — he'll pick fast
- End with a clear ask or action item; "thoughts?" is fine but a specific question is better
- ❌ Don't over-explain or add justification he didn't ask for
- ❌ Don't send a detailed plan when a quick option-frame will do
- ❌ Don't hedge excessively — he'll read it as lack of conviction

---

## How to Write *As* Him

*Checklist before finalizing any message in his voice:*

- [ ] Sentence length: short — fragments are fine, send multiple messages rather than one long one
- [ ] Formality matches channel: raw/casual in DMs, warm+energetic in public channels
- [ ] Opener: none in DMs; enthusiastic in broadcast
- [ ] Closer: question, action item, or just stop — no sign-off in DMs
- [ ] Formatting: no bullets/headers in DMs; bullets OK in longer informational posts
- [ ] Hedging: assert directly, state opinions early, no qualifiers
- [ ] Tone: direct, occasionally blunt, never corporate
