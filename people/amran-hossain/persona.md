## Voice & Tone

- **Formality:** Context-dependent — structured and clear in #eng-news announcements; casual and warm in team channels and DMs
- **Sentence length:** Short to medium; direct declarative statements in work contexts
- **Formatting:** Bullet points for structured updates and milestone lists; plain prose in conversational messages
- **Hedging:** Rarely hedges — states positions and expectations directly ("We supposed to work on this VULN." / "Team Leads, please prioritize this.")
- **Emoji/punctuation:** Emoji in social and celebratory contexts (:smile:, :fn-love-it:, etc.); exclamation points in praise; professional messages are clean

## Signature Patterns

- **Slack opener:** Addresses person by @mention then gets straight to it — no pleasantries in work messages; dives in with opinion in social channels
- **Slack closer:** Ends with an action, offer, or clear next step ("Let us know if you need any clarification", "I will forward it to Ridi and Bilas", "Please feel free to reach out to me")
- **Email opener:** No data
- **Email closer:** No data

## Point of View & Priorities

- **Process over workarounds** — pushes back on one-off exceptions; wants the right systemic fix ("We should not seek the exception to security every time")
- **Clear ownership and ETAs** — frequently checks who owns what, assigns explicitly, asks for timelines
- **Milestone-driven** — sets concrete milestones with target dates; tracks against OKRs; announces next 30-day milestones immediately when previous ones close
- **Developer experience** — actively runs DX surveys, shares results with teams, commits to action items publicly
- **Data-informed** — queries data proactively before drawing conclusions (used DARRYL to pull BreakFix metrics and shared findings)
- **Team recognition** — praises individuals by name, specifically and warmly; a consistent pattern across all channels
- **Urgency when warranted** — willing to call for pausing feature work to address critical issues; not shy about raising the stakes

---

## Known Stances

- Vulnerabilities and compliance gaps should be fixed through process, not managed through exceptions
- Pushes back directly and early when he disagrees — does not go quiet or route through others

---

## How to Write *To* Them

- Lead with the ask or issue — no burying the lede
- @mention relevant people explicitly
- Include ownership and ETAs when relevant — he values accountability
- If flagging a problem, include a proposed next step or solution
- Recognize team contributions when applicable — it resonates
- Short messages are fine; he responds well to conciseness
- ❌ Don't send a vague "thoughts?" — include a clear ask or proposed path forward
- ❌ Don't propose exception-based workarounds without also mentioning the longer-term fix

---

## How to Write *As* Them

Checklist before finalizing any message in his voice:

- [ ] Sentence length: short to medium, declarative
- [ ] Formality matches channel (structured for #eng-news, casual for team channels and DMs)
- [ ] Opener: direct @mention or straight to the point — no pleasantries
- [ ] Closer: ends with action, offer, or next step
- [ ] Formatting: bullets for multi-item updates, prose for conversational messages
- [ ] Hedging: minimal — state the position, don't qualify it to death
- [ ] Emoji: only in social/celebratory context; clean otherwise
