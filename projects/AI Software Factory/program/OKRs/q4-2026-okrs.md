# Q4 2026 OKR and Plan: Fai

*Fai is the name for the factory. This document defines the Q4 OKR and its supporting plan.*

*Source: Cory's Q4 proposal, adopted 2026-10-07. Replaces the earlier Fieldworks draft. KRs and milestones revised 2026-10-14 (see Key results).*

## Why this matters to the company

**Theme: improve engineering delivery through shared knowledge and agentic assistance.**

- Tooling alone has not moved throughput. Emerald is at roughly 1.4 PRs per engineer per week against a 3.5 target.
- Friction often starts before the ticket: finding the right data source, placing a model in the right layer, and recovering undocumented business logic.
- Work also slows after a PR is ready while people shepherd it through review and merge.

The factory addresses these constraints with companyOS as a shared knowledge layer, agents that take eligible issues through the existing engineering workflow, and measurement of cycle time and quality. The goal is not to maximize agent runs or PR volume; it is to complete useful engineering work faster and reliably.

## Objective

**Increase engineering delivery capacity by getting reliable changes shipped faster, with less human effort.**

## Key results

*Revised 2026-10-14 per Chuck. Three KRs and three milestones. The earlier "complete at least 20 issues" KR is now milestone M3, quality moved from KR3 to KR2, and a new company knowledge layer KR was added as KR3.*

**KR1. Issue cycle time is under 2 days, for Factory-created features.**
Median elapsed time from the Factory's first substantive work to an accepted, merged PR is under two calendar days. Carried over from the earlier draft: include review wait in the primary measure and track blocked time separately rather than excluding it.

**KR2. Factory changes meet quality guardrails.**
Change failure rate (CFR) below 5% and defect ratio below 5%. Carried over from the earlier draft: CFR applies to Factory-attributed production deployments; the escaped-defect ratio applies to completed Factory issues with a full observation window; no Sev1 or Sev2 escaped defects attributable to Factory changes.

**KR3. Company knowledge layer for agents.**
Agents have a shared company knowledge layer to draw on (companyOS). Measurable definition is not yet written; see open items.

**Measurement notes (carried over, still apply to KR1 and KR2):** Define eligible issues, acceptance criteria, issue start, Factory takeover, production attribution, and defect observation window before the pilot. Change failure rate is a deployment-level DORA metric; it is not measurable for changes that are only merged and never deployed. Calculate escaped-defect ratio over a fixed post-merge observation window and report the numerator and denominator with the percentage. Do not treat issues without a full observation window as defect-free.

**Scope implication:** M3 (20 tickets end to end) is the volume that feeds KR1 and KR2, so the eligible backlog and pilot capacity must support 20 completed tickets. Q4 does not commit to a direct measure of human hands-on time.

## High-level plan

**Foundation:** Agree on the issue cohort and metric definitions, then evaluate platform and harness options against one shared test. Start the walking skeleton in an existing sandbox while keeping the run contract portable.

**Knowledge and agent path:** Make meeting synthesis the first agent on the platform. Add agent creation by config, then the scaffolder, project sync, and staleness check. Use the Factory to create and maintain FNOS and the Engineering Senior Leadership OS. Validate that canonical knowledge can be found and that records link to source material.

**Factory pilot:** Route eligible issues from Linear through agents, Gitar, Sonar, review, and merge. Start with the Factory's own repos. Record every attempt and outcome, and use failures to improve the workflow. Scale only while cycle time and quality remain within the KRs.

**Out of scope for this OKR:** Full command center UI, upstream shaping and TDRs, additional OSs, a second repo target, and long-term platform ownership. Revisit these in the next planning cycle.

## Decision gate: what the platform spike must answer

- **AgentCore harness (AWS).** Verify cost attribution per agent and run, and fit with the fn-core sandbox image.
- **pi.** Verify MCP support and fit with the required run contract.
- **OpenCode.** Evaluate against the same shared test and run contract.
- **Combination to test.** Determine whether pi or OpenCode can run inside AgentCore, allowing hosting and tracing to be evaluated separately from the harness.
- **Cloud and workflow options.** Resolve AWS versus GCP and the Workato evaluation as part of the platform decision.

## Risks

- **Target volume.** The eligible backlog, issue mix, and pilot ramp must support M3 (20 tickets end to end).
- **Quality sample size.** A small number of Factory changes makes percentage rates volatile. Always report counts, and do not claim a stable failure rate from a handful of deployments.
- **Defect observation.** Late-quarter merges may not have a full post-merge observation window. Report them as pending rather than defect-free.
- **Deployment attribution.** If Factory changes are not deployed to production, DORA change failure rate is not available; use the defined escaped-defect measure and label the deployment metric not applicable.
- **Small team and ownership.** The team is Chuck, Cory, Mykhailo, and Tashfiq as time allows. ai-toolbox, agent hosting, and each OS need named owners. The plan assumes the team has capacity to support the pilot alongside other commitments.
- **Bootstrapping.** Early Factory output may need significant human rework. Humans merge all changes; failed or blocked runs should be recorded for operational analysis.
- **Scope.** The full Fai vision and full OS family exceed this OKR. Q4 focuses on the thin slice required to test delivery speed, quality, and the knowledge layer.

## Open items

1. **Eligible issue set:** confirm issue types, acceptance criteria, and enough eligible work to support M3 (20 tickets).
2. **Baseline:** confirm a comparable human issue cohort and the source of issue, review, deployment, and defect timestamps.
3. **Quality attribution:** confirm how Factory-attributed deployments and escaped defects will be identified, and which post-merge observation window will be used.
4. **KR3 and M2 definition:** write the measurable definition of "company knowledge layer for agents" and confirm what "CompanyOS built for Engineering" includes (FNOS, Engineering Senior Leadership OS, or both).
5. **Ownership:** name an owner for ai-toolbox, agent hosting, FNOS, and the Engineering Senior Leadership OS.
6. **Scope and participation:** confirm the first two OSs, where the Engineering Artifact Map lives, and whether Emerald, Core UI, and FN Core work remains outside the Factory pilot.
