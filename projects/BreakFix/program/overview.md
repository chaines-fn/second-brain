# Break/Fix Program Overview

## What Is Break/Fix?

Field Nation's Break/Fix (B/F) program builds an intelligent, automated break/fix fulfillment engine. Break/fix = reactive field service (something's broken, send a tech to fix it). High volume, low value, tight SLAs — 1-2 hour assignment windows, 14k+ WOs/month.

**Why it matters:** Competitive pressure, buyer demand for predictable fulfillment at scale. Moves FN beyond placement into managed service delivery.

## Three Teams / Three Workstreams

| Team | Workstream | Focus |
|------|-----------|-------|
| **SLATE** | Network Development (NetDev) | Recruiting & vetting providers into curated B/F networks |
| **GARNET** | Smart Dispatch & Fulfillment | Automated dispatch engine: tier-based, marketplace expansion, ranking |
| **MARBLE** | Parts Management | Parts logistics: tracking, returns, provider-added parts |
